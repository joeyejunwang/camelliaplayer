## Description

Camellia Player is a focused local media player for learning, practice, and close listening.

Open audio or video from Files, add a matching lyric file, and follow every line while the media plays. Repeat the current lyric automatically, jump directly to any line, or continue from your last-played lyric.

Features:

- Play local audio and video files
- Load same-name lyric files automatically
- Open SRT, WebVTT, ASS, and SSA files
- Repeat one lyric continuously
- Jump with the lyric list or slider
- Continue from the last-played lyric
- Show lyrics over video or in a scrollable list
- Continue audio playback in the background
- Keep media and lyric processing on your device

Camellia Player has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or account requirement.

Camellia Player does not require an account or network connection. To test, use the Files button to select one local media file and an optional same-name SRT, VTT, ASS, or SSA file. The repeat-current-lyric mode is enabled by default. All selected media remains on the device.
