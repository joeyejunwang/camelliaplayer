Camellia Player

Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Overview

Camellia Player is a local media player. The iOS app does not require an account and does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

Your media

Media and lyric files are opened only when you select them through the system file picker or place them in the app’s Documents folder. Playback and lyric processing happen on your device. Your selected files are not uploaded by Camellia Player.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the path, name, date, and last lyric position of the most recently played file so you can continue later. This information remains in the app’s local storage and can be removed by deleting the app.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Camellia Player does not include advertising, analytics, user tracking, or third-party account sign-in.
