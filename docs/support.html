Support

Need help with media playback, lyric files, or restoring your last lyric?

Contact

Email sighwang1900@hotmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, media format, and a short description of the problem.

Loading lyrics

Name the lyric file exactly like the media file, for example lesson.mp4 and lesson.srt. Select both files together in Files, or use the lyric button inside the player.

Supported lyric formats

Camellia Player supports SRT, WebVTT, ASS, and SSA lyric files.

Background playback

Audio can continue when Camellia Player is in the background. iOS may pause playback after an interruption from another app, a phone call, or an audio device change.
